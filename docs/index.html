Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

01. Introduction

Welcome to Insta In ("we," "our," "us," or the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy is designed to explain in deep detail how we collect, process, utilize, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related web properties.

By registering an account, logging in via Google Sign-in, or performing tasks within the Insta In platform, you explicitly consent to the data collection and usage practices described in this document. If you do not agree with any terms of this policy, please do not access or use the App.

Insta In is a community-driven promotion platform. Users can spend coins to launch Instagram Follower Campaigns or Sponsor Ad Visits, or earn coins by supporting other creators' profiles and visiting their business links.

02. Information We Collect

We collect information about you directly from your interactions, automatically through the application, and from third-party services like Google Authentication. The data collected is grouped as follows:

A. Registration & Profile Data

When you log in for the first time via Google Authentication, we retrieve your Google account profile information, which includes:

  • Your full name (Google Display Name)
  • Your primary registered Gmail address
  • Your Google account profile photo URL
  • A unique identifier token (Google UID)

B. Social Media Data

To enable Instagram follow tasks and verify task completions, we require and collect your Instagram credentials and link profiles:

  • Your Instagram username (e.g., @username)
  • Your public Instagram Profile URL
  • Interaction logs (profiles you have followed through our platform)

C. Payment & Settlement Data

For settlements, deposits, and withdrawal processing, we collect financial transaction credentials:

  • UPI ID (e.g., yourname@upi) for processing payout requests
  • UPI Phone Number linked to your UPI account for verification
  • Transaction receipts, amounts, deposit history, and Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) numbers submitted by you to verify manual bank/UPI deposits

D. System & Device Logs

We automatically log metadata from your mobile device to prevent multi-accounting and verify security:

  • Operating System version (Android/iOS) and device model
  • IP Address and network connection details
  • Active app sessions, clicks, timestamps, and navigation history

03. How We Use Your Data

We process your data strictly under the rules of necessity and consent. Here is how your information is used:

  • Service Provision: To create, manage, and display campaign tasks. This includes showing your Google name and profile photo to other users so they can confirm who they are following on Instagram.
  • Earning & Rewards Verification: To track coin balances, verify if an Instagram follow task or sponsored link visit was genuinely completed, and reward coins correctly.
  • Withdrawals Processing: To check withdrawal requests and route the corresponding monetary balance to your verified UPI ID or UPI phone number.
  • Anti-Abuse Operations: To prevent bots, fake clicks, immediate unfollowing, and coordinate security measures.

04. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers to ensure the core functions of our application work properly:

Firebase Services (Google LLC)

We host our database, user authentication services, and application logs on Google Firebase. Firebase complies with rigorous security standards. The specific Firebase sub-services used are:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages secure user login tokens safely without exposing your passwords to our servers.
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores profile details, task campaign history, deposit receipts, activity logs, and coin balances.
  • Firebase Storage: Stores submitted verification receipts or payment screenshots.

Instagram/Meta Platforms

When you click "Follow," we launch the Instagram application on your device to open the target profile. While we do not share your private Google credentials with Instagram, the meta-app links your follow actions publicly based on your public Instagram settings.

05. Anti-Fraud, Immediate Unfollows & Bans

To ensure fairness and authenticity for sponsors and creators who spend real money or effort on our app, we enforce strict anti-fraud tracking algorithms:

⚠️ CRITICAL RULE: Unfollowing profiles after claiming coin rewards is strictly prohibited. Our system performs cross-checks on withdrawal requests. Fake claims, immediate unfollows, or using dummy/bot accounts will result in immediate bans and permanent loss of all earnings.

To implement this, our app tracks:

  • Your account task history (profiles you followed through the App).
  • Verification timestamps when your account returns to the App via our background resume observer.
  • Random automatic audits matching the list of tasks completed by your account against the live following list on your linked Instagram profile.

06. Data Security & Retention

We employ administrative, physical, and electronic security measures to safeguard your information from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All database communication is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) transmission technology.

Data Retention Policy

We store your data as long as your account remains active in our system. If your account is banned for fraud or unfollowing, we retain your unique identifiers, UPI information, and transaction history indefinitely to prevent future registration and protect our system integrity.

For clean/active accounts, data is deleted or fully anonymized upon a valid account deletion request.

07. Your Legal Rights & Controls

You have control over how your data is accessed, modified, and deleted within Insta In:

  • Edit Profile Information: You can edit your display name, Instagram username, UPI ID, and UPI Phone Number at any time by navigating to the Edit Profile screen in the app.
  • Withdrawal Audits: You can request a complete history of your coin activity, deposits, and withdrawal transactions.
  • Account Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account and related records. To request deletion, please contact support at the email below. We will delete your information within 15 business days, except where we are legally required to retain transaction details for financial audits.

08. Children's Privacy

Insta In is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under 13. In the event that we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under age 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete that database entry immediately.
